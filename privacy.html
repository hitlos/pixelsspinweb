Who we are

PixelsSpin is a casual mobile game. This policy explains how the app and its third‑party partners handle data.

Data we handle

Collected by third parties (AdMob)

PixelsSpin uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the Google Advertising ID and device information for ad delivery, personalization (if enabled), frequency capping, and fraud prevention. See Google’s policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps
  • EU User Consent Policy (if applicable)

Collected by us

  • Game progress & coins — stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, or other personal identifiers beyond the advertising ID collected by AdMob.

Advertising (AdMob)

AdMob provides interest‑based and contextual ads. Depending on your region and settings, you may see a consent prompt or an in‑ad “Why this ad?”/“AdChoices” control to manage preferences. You can also reset your device’s advertising ID or disable ad personalization (see Your choices below).

Local storage & retention

  • Where stored: On your device only.
  • What stored: Coin balance and basic gameplay state.
  • How long: Until you uninstall the app or clear data.

Your choices

Reset game data

You can delete local game data any time:

  1. Open your device Settings → Apps → PixelsSpin.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data.

If provided in‑app, a Reset Game Data button will also clear local preferences.

Ads & advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Opt out of Ads Personalization”.
  • In‑ad controls: Use the AdChoices/Why this ad? options where available.

Children’s privacy

PixelsSpin is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required by local law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
